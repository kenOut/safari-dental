How it works

How a Dental Implant Is Built

Three parts replace one missing tooth, fitted one after another.

  1. The titanium post

    Placed in the jawbone, where the bone grows around it over the following months. It becomes the tooth's new root.

  2. The abutment

    A small connector fixed to the post once it has healed. It holds the new tooth.

  3. The crown

    A new tooth made to match the shape and colour of your own teeth, fixed on top.
