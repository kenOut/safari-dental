About Us

Professional and Personal Dental Care

Safadi Medical Hub opened at Spintex Plaza in 2020 and brings dentists, a maxillofacial surgeon and a primary care doctor together in one building, so most of your care happens in one place.

  • Dentists and a GP under one roof
  • Implant surgery in our own theatre
  • Invisalign-accredited dentist
  • Gentle care for children

Frequently Asked Questions

Anything else? Phone us during opening hours on 020 229 9995.

Contact the clinic
Is the clinic open on Fridays?

No. We open Monday to Thursday and on Saturday, 8:30 to 17:00. Friday and Sunday we are closed.

Does the clinic treat children?

Yes. We see children for check-ups, cleaning and preventive care.

Can I get Invisalign at the clinic?

Yes. Dr Naa Oyo Glover is accredited for Invisalign, and Dr Samuel Addo, our orthodontist, fits braces.

What do I do in a dental emergency?

Phone 020 229 9995 during opening hours and tell us what happened. We see urgent cases as soon as we can.

What are my options for a missing tooth?

An implant or a bridge. At your visit the dentist looks at the gap and the teeth around it and explains which suits you.

Is there a general doctor at the clinic?

Yes. Dr Hamza Basheer is the clinic's primary care physician.
