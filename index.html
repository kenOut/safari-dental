Questions before your visit

Anything else, phone us during opening hours.

020 229 9995

Is the clinic open on Fridays?

No. We open Monday to Thursday and on Saturday, 8:30 to 17:00. Friday and Sunday we are closed.

Does the clinic treat children?

Yes. We see children for check-ups, cleaning and preventive care.

Can I get Invisalign at the clinic?

Yes. Dr Naa Oyo Glover is accredited for Invisalign, and Dr Samuel Addo fits braces.

What do I do in a dental emergency?

Phone 020 229 9995 during opening hours and tell us what happened. We see urgent cases as soon as we can.

Is there a general doctor at the clinic?

Yes. Dr Hamza Basheer is the clinic's primary care physician.
